## Corrections made

### Arithmetic matroids

The representable arithmetic-matroid multiplicity is an **index/saturation multiplicity**. For a full-rank basis represented in (mathbb Z^r),
[
m(B)=|det(A_B)|.
]

The squared determinant (det(A_B)^2) belongs naturally to Cauchy–Binet identities and should not be presented as an interchangeable arithmetic-matroid convention.

### Graph Jacobian versus tropical Jacobian

The finite critical group
[
K(G)cong operatorname{Pic}^0(G)
]
is the **combinatorial graph Jacobian**.

The Jacobian of a metric graph / tropical curve is a real torus of dimension (eta_1). These objects are related by divisor theory and specialization, but they are not literally the same object.

### Discriminant groups

The dual lattice must be defined inside the real span of the lattice, not in the full ambient (mathbb R^E) unless the lattice has full ambient rank.

### Tree case

The inclusion
[
C+Zsubseteq mathbb Z^E
]
is not always strict. For a tree,
[
Z=0,qquad C=mathbb Z^E.
]

### Coloring targets

Ordinary Hadwiger remains the principal open-problem target.

Odd Hadwiger is **not** an open target: it was disproved in 2025. It is retained only as a negative-control / stress-test case.
